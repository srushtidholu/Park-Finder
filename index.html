    How it works

    Six steps, start to finish.

    1. Park and note your bayLook for the bay number painted on the pillar or floor.
    2. Open Save spotChoose Palladium Mall, Alpha Mall or TRP Mall.
    3. Enter the plate, floor, zone and bayZones and bay numbers change with the mall you pick.
    4. Tick optional servicesCar washing or EV charging, only if you need them.
    5. Go shoppingThe spot stays on your phone until you clear it.
    6. Search your plate on the way outYou get the floor, zone and bay. Unknown plates show “Vehicle not found”.

    Works offline

    Everything runs on your device. No account, no server, no signal needed in the basement. Install it to your home screen once and it opens like an app.

    Where your data lives

    Saved in this browser only. Clearing site data, or using another phone or browser, means the spot will not be found.
